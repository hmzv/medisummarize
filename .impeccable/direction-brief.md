# Frontend redesign: direction round

User confirmed: image mockups first, show options before implementation, and include experiment results in the app. Training runs in Kaggle. Surface mode: Operate.

Mechanism: a patient-scoped document workspace lets a reviewer compare a generated summary with the exact extracted source passages, while a separate experiments view reports measured OCR and summarization runs.

Scene: a student demonstrates document ingestion and source checking to FYP reviewers. Cultural home: clinical research and evidence review. First surface must prove that source text and generated claims remain distinguishable.

Category rut: generic medical dashboard with decorative KPI cards. Predictable opposite: dark futuristic AI cockpit. Neither is a candidate.

## Grounded candidates, ordered before presentation

1. Clinical conference identity: deep teal, cool mist, clear humanist type; compact sidebar and side-by-side evidence review, matched source markers as signature.
2. Medical atlas indexing: ink blue and pale cyan, indexed sans headings; familiar app shell with document thumbnails and source index.
3. University research poster program: aubergine, lavender, white; high contrast section labels and standard tables, linked section bands as signature.
4. Evidence journal typography: plum, pale lilac, white; precise editorial hierarchy inside a standard web application, a continuous plum source-selection rule joining summary and excerpt. This is the assigned direction (seed a0a6bc46).
5. Patient information booklet: forest, soft ivory, rust; generous readable measures in a standard review workspace and a quiet next-action strip.
6. Scientific conference wayfinding: cobalt, pale blue, amber; strong legible navigation labels and a small workflow-progress band.
7. Peer-review annotations: navy, ice, magenta; dense standard table and review panes with paired annotation anchors.

Families: publication typography (2,4,5), institutional graphic identities (1,3,6), annotation practice (7).

## Challenger judgments

All judgments compare audience identification and product clarity against Evidence journal. The Operate rules restrict the source world's contribution to type, palette, density, and one signature move; standard application layout and controls remain.

- Crouwel grid: declined on both axes; keep strict shared alignment as a discipline, without visible poster grid or enormous letters.
- Boarding pass/gate board: declined on both axes; keep stable row identity during processing updates.
- Centre-rail reference setting: competitive on audience identification, weaker product clarity; show as an alternate using readable serif evidence text, bone/ink/vermilion palette, and paired citation markers in a standard split-pane app.
- Monochrome product marketing: declined on both axes; keep proof adjacent to assertions, without marketing sections.
- Midnight transit diagram: declined on both axes; keep clear distinction between selected and inactive source markers without a rail-map interface.
- Split-flap concourse: declined on both axes; keep tabular status alignment without theatrical flap animation.

The assigned direction receives those five named raises. The top grounded candidate, Clinical conference, is the single pick alternate; its risk is familiar healthcare-software character.
